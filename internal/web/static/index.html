    Start from any single clue

    A username, a nickname, a phone number, an e-mail, a domain, an IP, a wallet, a company name or a pasted profile link. rectool collects everything that clue exposes, then follows the strongest new leads it finds.

    1. Collect — dozens of public sources in parallel.
    2. Attribute — profiles are scored so people who merely share a handle are not mixed in.
    3. Pivot — emails, domains, IPs and usernames found along the way are investigated in turn.
    4. Map — see everything as an overview, an intelligence tree and a relationship graph.
